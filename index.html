Research

Work in Progress

  • Public Procurement and the Labor Mix

    This paper examines how firms shape their labor mix choices when they receive Public procurement contracts. In particular, I study whether the Government could potentially use these contracts as a risk-insurance mechanism against future demand uncertainty.

  • Optimal Public Procurement Allocation: Balancing Sectoral Productivity and Competition

    With Basile Grassi and Manuel Lleonart-Anguix

    This paper examines how governments should allocate public procurement contracts, studying the trade-off between productive efficiency and competition and its implications for prices and welfare.
